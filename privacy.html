CloudDisk privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026.

Who uses it

CloudDisk is a personal tool used only by the owner of this GitHub account, with their own Google accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

CloudDisk requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the tool can see, create, change and delete only the Google Drive files it created itself. It cannot see any other files in the Drive.

Where data goes

  • Files move only between the owner's computer and the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared, or sent to any server or third party other than Google Drive itself.
  • There are no analytics, tracking or advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already stored in Google Drive stay there until the owner deletes them.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on this repository.
